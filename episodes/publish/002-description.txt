At half past two in the morning in Asia, a tiny transfer left the crypto exchange Bitget. Less than three hours later, about $388 million was gone, and Bitget says not a single private key was stolen. At 19:05 UTC the alarm rang and every customer was locked out. The money kept leaving for another two hours.

A crypto heist and cybercrime documentary: how attackers got in through the security products Bitget bought to keep them out, why the signing machine kept running after the alarm, the fingerprints they left on the blockchain, where the money went, and who the analysts think was behind it. Plus the irony at the start of it all: in February 2025, Bitget was the exchange that rushed to rescue Bybit.

STATUS AS OF LATE NOVEMBER 2026: no government has attributed the Bitget hack. Chainalysis, Elliptic and TRM Labs lean towards North Korean hackers; Bitget's CEO cited preliminary indicators. The hacked security vendor has not been named. No one has been charged. Details may have changed after publication.
