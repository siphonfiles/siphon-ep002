## Meaning
- **53** `Mandiant只把攻击者的特权访问认定在9月24日` → `Mandiant只把攻击者的特权访问追溯到9月24日`. "认定在" reads as "Mandiant determined it was on 24 Sep". The English means Mandiant only takes it back as far as 24 Sep, no earlier. "追溯到" says that.
- **138** `汇集了约1000个零碎的币` → `汇集了约1000笔零散的小额资金`. "零碎的币" is vague and reads oddly. The new wording makes it clear these are ~1,000 small separate amounts (inputs), as the producer asked.
- **139** `而该钱包此前最大的一笔交易，只用了54个。` → `而此前，该钱包最大的一笔交易汇集了54笔。` Uses the same counter as 138. Also drops the added "只" ("only"), which the English doesn't have.
- **142** `没有人在盯着它们` → `没有人在留意它们`. The English is "watching *for* them" (nobody was on the lookout), not "watching them".
- **181** `但分析师都指向同一方向` → `但分析师指向同一方向`. Drops the added "都" ("all"), which the English doesn't have in this cue.
